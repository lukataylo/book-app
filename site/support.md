# Support

Epigrapha is made by one person. The fastest way to get help is to open an
issue — it is read directly, and you do not need to explain who you are.

## Get help

- **Bugs and questions:** [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/lukataylo/book-app/issues/new)
- **Report a content or rights concern:** open an issue titled
  `Content concern: <book title>`. Rights holders can also use the same
  link; every catalog title is a work published before 1930 whose
  copyright has expired, and the summaries are original prose, but if you
  believe a title should not be listed it will be reviewed and, if
  appropriate, withdrawn in the next release.

## Common questions

**Do I need an API key?**
No. The catalog, reader, listening, speed reading, highlights and search
all work with no key, no account and no network.

**What is the key for, then?**
Optional cloud transformations — compressing, expanding or re-styling a
book. They use your own Anthropic account, billed by Anthropic directly.
Epigrapha never marks up or collects any cloud cost. Nothing is sent until
you grant permission, and you can withdraw it in Settings → Privacy.

**Where are my books stored?**
On your device, and in your own private iCloud. There is no Epigrapha
server. See the [Privacy Policy](/book-app/privacy/).

**A book won't import.**
Epigrapha reads EPUB and PDF. Other formats (MOBI, AZW, KFX) need
converting first — Calibre does this for free.
